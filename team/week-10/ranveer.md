## 1. Bug bash (Mon 14 Dec, 30 min)

On your own phone, with the test account Meghna gives you: ask the questions your
interviewees asked, try to make it invent a case, upload a public document (if uploads are
on). Every problem: a screenshot and one line, to Meghna.

## 2. The invite (Wed 16 Dec)

> Subject: Your early access to [product name from Shaan]
>
> Dear [Name], thank you for helping us shape this. Your early access opens on **Monday 4 January**. Sign in at [address] with **this email address** (only invited addresses work).
>
> Before your first question, please read the one-page guide (attached). The most important things: it covers Supreme Court judgments and Central Acts up to [snapshot date]; a tick means the cited record **exists in our dataset**, not that it's good law; and it's information, **not legal advice**.
>
> Please join a 30-minute group call in the first week: [slot options]. And when an answer is wrong, the "Report a wrong answer" button is the most useful thing you can press.
>
> Regards, Ranveer

Shaan checks the wording before it goes out. **The product name in the email is whatever the
trademark result allows.**

## 3. Kickoff calls (Fri 18 Dec)

Five or six group calls in **4 to 8 January**, 5 or 6 people each. Book them now. Shaan or
Meghna joins each one to show the screen.

## 4. The support plan (Fri 18 Dec)

`team/beta/support-plan.md`:
- **Where beta users ask for help**: the in-app feedback form first; an email address for
  anything private. **Not a group chat**: people would share client details there
- **How fast you reply**: same working day
- **Anything about a made-up citation or someone else's data goes to Shaan within the hour**
- A short survey at the **end of the beta**, including the price question for launch gate (b):
  *would you pay ₹499 a month?* (advocates) and ₹199 (students)

## 5. Go/no-go (Fri 18 Dec, all four)

Bring: how many of the 30 are still keen, anything they've been told, and whether you'd be
comfortable handing it to an advocate you respect. **Say so if you wouldn't.**

## 6. Security test
